# Mouth Synth Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 10, 2026

ToppyMotion ("we" or "us") handles information associated with Mouth Synth for iPhone and iPad (the "app") as described below.

## 1. About the app

Mouth Synth is a musical instrument that uses the front camera to recognize mouth movements without requiring a voice. Play mode controls pitch, while Sequence mode controls rhythms and phrases. Some sounds require a one-time Pro in-app purchase. No app account or login is required.

## 2. Camera images and face data

With your camera permission, the app uses Apple's on-device image analysis, including Vision, to detect face and mouth positions, lip outlines, mouth opening, and related information ("face data"). This information controls pitch, sound triggering, sequences, and on-screen visuals in response to mouth movements.

Camera images and face data are processed temporarily on your device. We do not save them as a face database or training data, or send them to our servers, external AI services, GameAnalytics, or advertising providers. They are not used for authentication, identifying individuals, profiling, or advertising.

## 3. Audio, recording, and the photo library

Sounds are synthesized on your device. The app does not use microphone input or collect or record your voice or surrounding audio. Voice-like sounds are also synthesized.

The current version does not record, save, or share camera footage or performance videos. It does not read existing photos or videos or request permission to add items to the photo library. If you use the operating system's screen recording or another app to record or share a performance, that feature or service handles the information under its own terms.

## 4. Information stored on your device

The app stores preferences such as selected sounds, pitch range, mouth sensitivity, scale, effects, tempo, voice mode, onboarding completion, and your analytics choice. If you consent to analytics, it also stores a randomly generated analytics identifier.

These preferences preserve your choices and support app operation. We do not send face or mouth measurements, the individual notes you play, audio, or detailed sensitivity and effect settings to analytics. Section 6 explains the usage information, including selected sounds and modes, that may be shared.

## 5. In-app purchases

Mouth Synth Pro is a one-time, non-consumable purchase that unlocks additional sounds. Purchases and restoration use Apple's App Store and StoreKit. The app checks verified purchase information supplied by Apple to determine access.

Apple processes payments. We do not receive your card number, Apple Account password, or similar payment or authentication credentials. Purchase receipts are not sent to GameAnalytics. Apple's handling of information is covered by [Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

## 6. Optional usage analytics and GameAnalytics

Only if you consent to sharing usage information, we use GameAnalytics to understand usage and improve usability, sounds, and the musical experience. Sharing is optional. Declining does not restrict app features; the usual purchase requirement still applies to Pro sounds.

With your consent, the app sends:

- Interaction and usage information, such as selected sounds and sequence sounds, switching between Play and Sequence modes, starting a performance, usage duration, first sound activation, and viewing the Pro offer.
- A randomly generated analytics identifier used to group activity from the same installation across sessions.
- Technical information sent by the GameAnalytics SDK, such as device and identifier information, operating system, app version, language, and sessions. Network communication exposes an IP address to the recipient, which is used to derive approximate location.

Camera footage, face images, face and mouth measurements, audio, and performance videos are not sent. Activity from before consent or while sharing is disabled is not sent later. GameAnalytics is not started if you decline sharing. If you previously consented, disabling sharing stops submission of new usage events.

The app has no ads or advertising SDKs. It does not use advertising identifiers or track activity across other companies' apps or websites for advertising purposes.

## 7. Service providers, retention, and international processing

GameAnalytics and its service providers process analytics on our behalf, potentially outside your country of residence. Retention and deletion follow the app-improvement purpose, GameAnalytics' data processing agreement and retention policies, and our service settings.

GameAnalytics currently describes retention of event-level data for up to 12 months, player-level aggregates for up to one year, and truncated IP addresses for up to 30 days. Aggregated statistics that do not identify individuals may be kept longer for historical trends. For details, including changes to the provider's policies and agreements, see the [GameAnalytics Privacy Notice](https://www.gameanalytics.com/trust/privacy-notice) and [Developer Policy](https://www.gameanalytics.com/trust/privacy-faq).

This policy page is hosted on GitHub Pages. When you visit it, GitHub may log your IP address and related information for security. This is separate from optional in-app analytics. See the [GitHub General Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

## 8. Permissions, withdrawing consent, and deletion

You can change camera permission in your device's Settings app. Mouth-controlled performance is unavailable without camera access.

To stop analytics, open Sound settings at the top right of the performance screen, select "Privacy settings", and turn off "Share app usage". Stopping sharing does not immediately delete information already sent.

Deleting the app removes its local data, including preferences and the analytics identifier. Offloading the app may retain data. Manage operating-system backups through the relevant system settings. If you saved or shared videos using an earlier version, manage or delete them at their destination.

Deleting the app does not automatically delete analytics already sent to GameAnalytics or purchase records held by Apple. Contact us using Section 9 for requests about access, correction, or deletion of submitted information. We will respond under applicable law and provider procedures to the extent we can identify the relevant information.
